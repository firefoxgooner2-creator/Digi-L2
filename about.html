Relevant implications

Accessibility and copyright

This website follows accessibility principles by using readable fonts, strong colour contrast, clear navigation, and descriptive alt text on images. This helps users with visual impairments and makes the site easier for all visitors to use.

All images used on the site are selected carefully to respect copyright. We use original, appropriately licensed, or ethically sourced media, and we give credit where required.

Design standards

Consistency and usability

Consistent fonts, colours, navigation, and layout help users feel comfortable and reduce confusion. The design uses clear headings, contrast, repetition, and spacing to keep the website predictable and easy to understand.

  • Navigation remains easy to find on every page.
  • Links use clear contrast and visible hover effects.
  • Content is structured with headings, lists, and readable spacing.
