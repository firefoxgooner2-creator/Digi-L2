Equipment

Ready for play

What players wear

Field hockey requires protective equipment to keep players safe and comfortable while moving quickly around the pitch. The right gear also improves control and confidence while playing.

  • Stick and hard ball
  • Shin guards and a mouthguard
  • Goalkeepers wear extra padding, gloves and leg guards

Game basics

Using the equipment correctly

Players use the flat face of the stick to control the ball. Proper technique helps players pass, receive, and shoot more accurately while staying within the rules of the game.

  • Use the flat side to play the ball.
  • Keep the stick in control at all times.
  • Protective gear helps reduce the risk of injury.
