Quick Facts

Learn the game

How the sport works

  • 11 players take the field for each team in outdoor hockey, including the goalkeeper.
  • Four 15-minute quarters are played in elite matches.
  • Only the flat side of the stick should be used to play the ball.
  • Goals are scored when the ball is played from inside the shooting circle.

Team roles

Positions

Each team works together with different roles. Attackers create chances, midfielders connect the play, and defenders protect the goal. The goalkeeper is the only player allowed to use their body to stop the ball inside the circle.

  • Forwards aim to score and create space.
  • Midfielders support both defence and attack.
  • Defenders block shots and protect the circle.
